About Our Factory

Modern Production, Skilled People, Reliable Delivery

Louietex was founded in 2013 by two visionaries whose foresight and business acumen paved the way for growth and expansion beyond the apparel industry. Today, Louietex serves a customer base across continents with a diverse product range and a strong team of skilled textile professionals.

The company operates through dedicated divisions including Merchandising and Marketing, Quality Assurance and Control, Research and Development, and Logistical Support. Louietex also uses a Management Information System to support online monitoring, information retrieval, and smooth workflow control.

Fabric Development

Seasonal Fabrics, Colours & Fashion Forecasting

Louietex develops new fabric designs before every collection through technically qualified textile engineers. Fabric ranges include yarn-dyed checks, stripes, dobby, seersuckers, viscose, and georgettes.
